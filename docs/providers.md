# Choosing an AI provider

> [简体中文](providers.zh-Hans.md)

This app has no server. Every report is generated by **an AI service you
choose**, called directly from your device with **your** key.

That makes the choice yours — and it matters more here than in most apps,
because of what gets sent.

## Read this before picking a "free" one

The payload is your honest self-assessment and your daily record of what you
avoided. It is roughly the most personal thing you could hand to a third
party.

Several free tiers are free precisely because **your prompts become training
data**, sometimes with human review. That is a fair trade for asking a chatbot
to write an email. It is a much worse trade for this.

So the list in Settings is **sorted by privacy, not by price.**

## The options

Verified 2026-08-24. Policies and free tiers change — check before relying on
any of this.

### Best: a model on your own machine

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ollama** | `http://localhost:11434/v1` |
| **LM Studio** | `http://localhost:1234/v1` |

Free, no key, no sign-up, and **nothing leaves your network**. The privacy
question stops being about trust and becomes a fact of the setup.

The cost is that you need a reasonably capable computer, and smaller local
models are weaker at following the required JSON schema — if reports fail,
the diagnostics panel will show you exactly what came back.

### Free with a real privacy policy

| | Free tier | Data policy |
|---|---|---|
| **[Groq](https://console.groq.com)** | yes | Zero data retention; prompts are discarded once the response returns, and are not used for training |
| **[Cerebras](https://cloud.cerebras.ai)** | 1M tokens/day, no card | Inputs and outputs are not retained and not used for training or fine-tuning |

These are the two to start with if you do not want to run anything locally.
Note that Cerebras' free tier caps context at 8K tokens — long report ranges
may get truncated, which the app will tell you about explicitly.

### Paid, but does not train on you

| | Data policy |
|---|---|
| **OpenAI** | API data is not used for training; retained briefly for abuse monitoring |
| **DeepSeek** | We have not verified their policy — read it yourself before using it |

### Free, but your notes may become training data

| | |
|---|---|
| **Google Gemini free tier** | Google reserves the right to use free-tier inputs and outputs to improve its products, including human review and inclusion in future training sets. The **paid** tier does not. |
| **OpenRouter free models** | OpenRouter itself does not log by default, but many of the upstream providers behind *free* models require logging and/or training to be enabled |

They are in the list because hiding options would be patronising, and because
for some people the trade is acceptable. But please make it a decision rather
than an accident — and consider whether you would be comfortable with a
stranger reading the entries you are about to submit.

## A note on model names

Model IDs go stale. Groq deprecated `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` in June 2026,
for example. The presets fill in a base URL (stable) and a suggested model
(not stable). If a model name is wrong, the provider returns a 4xx and the
diagnostics panel shows their error verbatim — edit the model field in
Settings and try again.

The app deliberately does **not** fetch the provider's model list. That would
mean a second network request, and
[data-flow.md](data-flow.md) promises there is no traffic at all until you ask
for a report. That promise is worth more than saving you one paste.
